About NGO Hope

Learn more about our mission, vision, and the people behind the cause.

Our Mission

Founded in 2010, NGO Hope was created with a simple goal: to ensure that no person suffers from treatable blindness or basic illness due to a lack of funds.

We partner with professional doctors and dedicated volunteers to organize community-led camps, providing free screenings and essential care.

Our Vision

"A world where healthcare is a right, not a privilege, and where every individual has the opportunity to lead a healthy and dignified life."
